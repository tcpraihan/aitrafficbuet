About the project

Dhaka's intersections are still largely run on fixed timers or manual control, while the traffic is some of the most mixed and variable in the world: buses, cars, CNG auto-rickshaws, cycle-rickshaws, motorbikes and pedestrians share the same road. This project measures what is actually happening at every approach with cameras and on-site AI, then lets the signal controller give green time where it is needed.
